# Findings and Limitations

The observations below are reported in the July 2025 study document; they have not been recalculated from raw experimental data.

## HN2 observations

The report describes maintained viability up to 1 µM lovastatin at 24 hours, with lower viability at 2.5 and 5 µM. At 48 hours, it describes a viability decrease from 0.5 µM onward.

It also describes approximately **40% cholesterol depletion at 5 µM after 24 hours** and approximately **30% at the same concentration after 48 hours**. These are approximate report-level observations, not newly extracted effect estimates. The report states that the difference between exposure durations requires further investigation.

## Neuro2a observations

The report describes reduced cholesterol at both 24- and 48-hour exposure durations. Its Neuro2a figure caption provides n=4 and mean ± SEM but does not specify the same significance annotations as the HN2 figures. This summary does not assign unreported p-values or quantitative effect sizes to Neuro2a results.

## Interpretation

The findings concern cholesterol content and viability in cultured neuronal cell models. Effects on membrane-protein dynamics, GPCR signaling, receptor conformation, diffusion, and trafficking are discussed as future research directions. They were not directly established by the measurements summarized here.

Protein normalization is important when interpreting cholesterol under conditions that also affect cell viability. Exact raw values and control/replicate annotations would be needed for a more detailed assessment.

## Limitations

- Cultured cell-line findings do not establish human neurological or clinical outcomes.
- Only the recorded exposure conditions and measured endpoints support the conclusions.
- Raw experimental readings and complete statistical files are not included.
- Multiple concentrations and time points were compared; adjustment across those comparisons is not documented in the supplied summary.
- Additional membrane-function and signaling experiments are needed to test the proposed mechanisms.
