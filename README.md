# Cholesterol Depletion in Lovastatin-Treated Neuronal Cells

**An experimental study of cholesterol content and cell viability in Neuro2a and HN2 cells following lovastatin exposure.**

[Methods](docs/METHODS.md) · [Findings and limitations](docs/FINDINGS.md) · [References and credits](docs/REFERENCES.md)

## Overview

This summer research project at **CSIR-CCMB, Hyderabad (2025)** investigated how lovastatin exposure affected cholesterol content in two neuronal cell models: **Neuro2a (N2a)** and **HN2**.

**Researcher:** [Jamyang Ugyen Tshomo](https://github.com/JamyangUT). I carried out the project during my summer internship under the supervision of **Prof. Amitabha Chattopadhyay**, with guidance from **Dr. Shishu Pal Singh**.

## Research question

How do lovastatin concentration and exposure duration affect cholesterol content and viability in neuronal cell models?

## Experimental approach

- Culture Neuro2a and HN2 cells and expose them to increasing lovastatin concentrations for **24 or 48 hours**.
- Assess cell viability with the **MTT assay**.
- Quantify protein content using the **BCA assay**.
- Measure cholesterol using the **Amplex Red Cholesterol Assay**, normalized to protein content.
- Analyze and visualize results with **GraphPad Prism 10**.

The report describes **four biological replicates and three technical replicates**, with results presented as mean ± SEM. Technical replicates are repeated measurements and do not increase the biological sample size.

## Main findings

- Lovastatin exposure reduced cholesterol content in both neuronal cell models.
- In HN2 cells, the report describes greater viability sensitivity after 48 hours than after 24 hours.
- The results support investigating how cholesterol depletion relates to neuronal membrane organization and function. Specific receptor dynamics and signaling changes were proposed as follow-up work, rather than directly measured endpoints.

## Tools and skills

**Cell culture · MTT · BCA · Amplex Red · Fluorescence measurement · Protein normalization · GraphPad Prism · Experimental data interpretation**

## Research status

This repository documents the completed internship study from the July 2025 report. Original plate readings, calibration files, and Prism analysis files are not included, so the measurements and statistical tests cannot be recomputed from the materials in this repository.

The findings concern cultured cell models under the study conditions. They do not establish clinical effects of statin use or outcomes in people with neurodegenerative disease.
